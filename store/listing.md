## English (default)

**Title:** Afnon LG Lite

**Description:**

Afnon LG Lite brings the prayer day to your LG TV: the next prayer and a live countdown inside a mihrab arch,
the six prayer times of the day, the Hijri and Gregorian dates, and the Qibla from your city.

- The adhan at prayer time, over any app or HDMI input, with 16 voices and a special one for Fajr.
- A reminder before each prayer, on screen and spoken, and a warning before the current prayer's time ends.
- Each time can be adjusted by minutes.
- Friday and Ramadan modes: Jumu'ah, iftar and suhoor.
- Quran radio stations, and live video from Makkah and Madinah.
- 192 cities in 43 countries, each with the calculation method its mosques use. The times are calculated on
  the TV, without the internet.
- Arabic first, with English. Night mode, and a dim screen while you listen.

No accounts, ads or tracking. Afnon LG Lite is the LG version of Afnon by egyjs, maintained by alequab.
